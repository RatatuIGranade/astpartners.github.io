Commands Overview

Partner (ConCord) limited to CONCORD_CONNECT_IDS (admins included)

Show current partner status (level, PTS, guild link, next level progress).
/partner_me
Display your ConCord identity on this guild.
/partner_reps name:"ServerName"
List current representatives for the partner.
/feedback message:"..." partner_name:"ServerName"
Send feedback/report to ASTERION (#partners-feedback).

Quick health-check for ConCord on this guild.
/partner_help
Show available partner commands.

Role Permissions

Access Model

Admins (ASTERION) — full access to all admin & partner commands.
Partner Admins (ConCord) — limited to informational and feedback commands.

  • ADMIN_ROLE_IDS — can run: /partner_add, /partner_remove, /partner_addrep, /addpts, /setlevel, /migrate, /partner_leaderboard + all partner commands.
  • CONCORD_CONNECT_IDS — can run: /partner_info, /partner_me, /partner_reps, /feedback, /status, /partner_help.
  • Admins implicitly pass partner checks.
PTS & Levels System

Scoring & Progress

Thresholds are configurable via LEVELS_JSON in your .env. Default:

{
  "1": 8000,
  "2": 20000,
  "3": 40000
}

Partners see current level, total PTS, and remaining PTS to the next level inside /partner_info.

Auto Voice Activity Tracking

How it accrues points

  • Every minute ConCord scans voice channels of each partner guild.
  • Counts human participants (bots ignored).
  • Adds PTS_PER_VC_MINUTE × participants to the partner’s PTS.
  • Message activity can also grant PTS with PTS_PER_MESSAGE.

All values are configurable via .env.

About ConCordConnect

What is ConCordConnect?

A secure role layer and telemetry bridge for ASTERION partners. It enforces least-privilege access for partner admins while giving ASTERION full operational control. ConCordConnect powers auto-PTS, feedback routing, and multi-guild command sync.
